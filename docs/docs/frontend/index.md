# PHAWM Workbench User Manual  

The PHAWM Workbench allows a group or individual, without expertise in AI, to audit an AI application. The benefits and research underpinning this approach are outlined in the PHAWM Methodology. For more information about the PHAWM project and the research underpinning this Workbench please visit phawm.org 

The manual lays out a structured, step‑by‑step process for auditing an AI application using the PHAWM Workbench. After logging in and reviewing the audit information provided by the instigator, auditors begin with the **AI Application Overview**, which supplies essential context such as the system’s purpose, data sources, model architecture, deployment environment, and legal framework. Once this background is understood, auditors move to the **Audit Criteria**, where they review predefined Events—possible positive or negative outcomes of using the AI system—and evaluate each one by assigning valence, likelihood, and magnitude. They then examine or create Metrics that measure whether these Events occur, setting thresholds and selecting which metrics to include in the audit. Participants also have the opportunity to create their own Events.  

The process continues with **Performance Analysis**, where auditors analyse each selected metric using available system data or by uploading their own data for evaluation. For every metric, they provide a recommendation—pass, fail, or inconclusive—along with justification. Finally, the **Audit Recommendation** stage compiles all metric‑level recommendations, allowing the auditor to make an overall judgment about whether the AI application passes, fails, or remains inconclusive. The auditor can add final feedback before submitting the completed audit. This structured workflow ensures that participants without any AI expertise can systematically assess AI applications.  

###Getting Started 

The audit instigator will send you a link to the workbench along with your password, which you can use along with your email address to login. Once you are logged in, visit your user **Profile** page by clicking the following symbol in the top right corner of the page and make sure to change your password to something secure.  

<img src="media/logo_person.png" alt="Image - Person" width="1000" style="display: block; margin: 0 auto">

You also have the option of providing some additional details that may be of interest to the Audit Instigator. Providing sensitive data, such as your **Gender** or **Ethnicity**, is optional, but could be useful for the Audit Instigator. There may be instances where it is useful to know about the concerns raised by someone from a specific demographic group, this will help to ensure a diversity of voices being heard.
